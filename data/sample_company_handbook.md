# Northstar Studio: Sample Employee Handbook

This fictional handbook is provided only as a safe sample knowledge base for the RAG Knowledge Assistant demo. It does not describe a real company or provide legal advice.

## Vacation requests

Employees should submit vacation requests to their manager at least five business days before the first requested day off. The manager reviews the request and confirms approval or denial in writing. Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved.

## Expense reimbursement

Employees should submit expense reports with an itemized receipt within 30 calendar days of the purchase. The Finance team reviews complete reports. Reimbursement is issued through the next regular payroll after approval.

## Password security

Employees must use a unique password for each company account and must not share passwords. If an employee suspects that a password has been exposed, they should change it immediately and notify the IT help desk.

## Remote work equipment

Employees may request a company headset or keyboard from the IT help desk. Equipment requests are subject to availability. The handbook does not specify a delivery date or guarantee approval.
